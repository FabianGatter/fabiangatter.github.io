Ball Fusion — Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-28

Ball Fusion is a puzzle game. This policy explains what data the game uses, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the iOS and Android apps and to the web version.

Data stored on your device

Your games, best scores, statistics, daily challenge streak, settings and the profile you enter (a name of up to 16 characters and, if you choose, a country) are stored on your device. This data stays on your device unless you use the leaderboard.

Leaderboard

If you add a name, the game creates an anonymous account for you (no email or password) and sends your name, a random four-character tag, your country if you chose one, and your scores to our leaderboard. Other players can see your name, tag, country and scores on the leaderboard.

The leaderboard is hosted by Supabase in the European Union (Ireland). We keep leaderboard data while your account exists. You can delete your account, and with it your leaderboard entry, in the app (see "Deleting your account" below).

Sign in with Apple (optional)

You can link your game account to your Apple ID (Profile or Settings → Account) to keep your coins and progress when you reinstall the app or change your iPhone. Apple then gives us a stable user identifier and, if you allow it, your email address or a private relay address ("Hide My Email"). Our authentication provider Supabase stores them in the European Union only to recognise your account. We do not use the email address for marketing and never show it to other players. Deleting your account deletes them and asks Apple to revoke the link.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center on your iPhone, your best scores and achievements are also sent to Apple Game Center under your Game Center profile. Apple’s privacy policy applies to Game Center.

Analytics (only with your consent)

With your consent we use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played, for example how many games are started, how long they last and which screens are used. The events contain counts and fixed choices only, never your name or anything you type, and analytics never uses the advertising ID. Google may process this data on our behalf. Where the law requires consent we ask first; everywhere you can turn it off in Settings → Share anonymous usage data.

Ads (only where shown, with your choice respected)

The game may show ads from Google AdMob: an optional video you can choose to watch for a reward, and a full-screen ad between games at most every few games. Ads are never shown while you play. Before ads are shown we ask for your consent where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), and on iPhone the system asks whether apps may track you. If you do not agree, you get non-personalized ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers (the advertising ID; on iPhone only if you allow tracking) and approximate location to show and measure ads; see Google’s policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Ads are limited to content suitable for all ages (rated G).

When you watch an optional video for coins, AdMob tells our server that the video was completed (server-side verification) so the coins can be credited. For this the game passes your anonymous account id to AdMob with the ad request.

Purchases and coins

Purchases (Remove Ads and coin packs) are handled by the App Store; we never see your payment details. To credit coins, the game sends the signed purchase receipt to our server, which checks it with Apple and records the purchase in your coin history. Your coin balance and coin history (what you earned, bought and spent, and when) are stored with your anonymous account on Supabase in the European Union and are used to run the coin system and to prevent fraud. We keep this history while your account exists; what happens when you delete your account is described below.

Deleting your account

In the iPhone and Android app you can delete your account at any time: Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes, on our server, your anonymous account, your leaderboard name, tag and country, your scores and replays, your league results, your friends and friend code, your coin balance and your coin history. If you signed in with Apple, the game also asks Apple to revoke its access to your Apple ID. This cannot be undone: coins, including coins you bought, cannot be restored.

Records of coin pack purchases and refunds (the product, the number of coins, the store’s transaction id, the date and the coin balance at that time) are kept after the deletion without any link to your account, because we need them for our accounting and to handle refunds the App Store reports to us. They contain no name or other profile data.

Data that only ever stayed on your device (statistics, settings) stays there until you reset it or remove the app. Remove Ads belongs to your App Store or Google Play account and can be restored after the deletion. On the web version, or if you cannot use the app, email us with your name and tag and we delete your account for you.

Children

Ball Fusion is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children beyond the optional leaderboard name, and we ask ad partners for content suitable for all ages.

Your rights

  • See or delete the data on your device: Settings → Reset stats, or remove the app.
  • Delete your account with your leaderboard entry and coin history: Profile → Delete account in the app (on the web: email us with your name and tag).
  • Change your consent for personalized ads: Settings → Privacy choices (shown where the law requires a consent choice). Elsewhere, use the iPhone setting Privacy & Security → Tracking, or Ads settings on Android.
  • Turn analytics off: Settings → Share anonymous usage data.
  • If you are in the EEA or the UK you may also complain to your data protection authority.
