Common questions

  • How do I play? Pull back from the ball at the bottom and release to shoot. Equal numbers merge. See How to Play in the app.
  • What is the Daily Challenge? Everyone gets the same balls each day (UTC). Your first run is your ranked score; retries are practice.
  • How does the streak work? Finish a daily run each day. You can miss one day a week and keep your streak.
  • How do I change my name? Settings → Profile → Edit.
  • How do I delete my account? In the app: Profile → Delete account. This also deletes your leaderboard entry and your coins. On the web, email us with your name and tag.
